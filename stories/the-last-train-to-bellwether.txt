---
title: The Last Train to Bellwether
tags: mystery, speculative
date: 2026-02-11
excerpt: The 11:40 has not appeared on any timetable for sixty years. Tonight, it is waiting for Jonah.
---
The station clock had stopped at eleven forty, though Jonah's phone insisted it was nearly midnight.

He was alone on the platform when the train arrived without sound. Its windows glowed amber. Behind each one sat a passenger wearing clothes from a different decade, all of them looking straight ahead.

The conductor stepped down. “Bellwether?” she asked.

Jonah looked at the paper ticket he had found that morning tucked inside his mother's final letter. The destination was printed in faded purple ink.

“I don't know,” he said. “Is that where this goes?”

The conductor smiled as if he had asked the wrong question. “It's where it stops.”

Behind him, the station exit opened onto the life he already knew. Ahead, a warm carriage waited, smelling faintly of coal smoke and summer rain.

Jonah climbed aboard.
