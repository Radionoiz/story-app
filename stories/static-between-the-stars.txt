---
title: Static Between the Stars
tags: science fiction, hope
date: 2025-08-16
excerpt: Alone at a lunar listening post, a radio operator hears a melody no human being has ever played.
---
For eleven months, Soren listened to the universe say nothing.

The lunar array collected every murmur that crossed the dark: the sigh of distant quasars, the magnetic storms of Jupiter, the old mechanical gossip of satellites. His job was to listen for something that did not belong.

At 03:12 station time, a melody appeared on channel seven.

Five notes rose through the static. They paused, then repeated.

Soren checked the equipment. He checked the relay traffic and the catalog of known signals. He even checked the recordings his daughter sent from her piano lessons on Earth.

The five notes came again.

Protocol required silence. Instead, Soren found the little keyboard beneath his bunk and played five notes in reply.

For eight minutes, nothing happened.

Then channel seven filled with sound—not speech, not music exactly, but something vast and bright unfolding note by note. Across the station, dormant monitors woke. The empty coffee cup beside his hand trembled.

Soren laughed. The sound startled him more than the signal had.

He opened a channel to Earth.

“Someone is out there,” he said. “And I think they're glad we answered.”
