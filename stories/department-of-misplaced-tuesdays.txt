---
title: The Department of Misplaced Tuesdays
tags: humor, speculative
date: 2025-11-07
excerpt: Priya's missing afternoon turns up in a government office between a lost umbrella and the summer of 1987.
---
Priya noticed the missing Tuesday when Wednesday arrived too early.

She had gone to sleep on Monday with a presentation unfinished and awakened to seventeen concerned messages asking why she had missed it.

The Department of Misplaced Time occupied a narrow office above a bakery. A clerk in a yellow cardigan listened patiently while Priya explained.

“Tuesday afternoon, approximately one to four,” Priya said. “Possibly later.”

The clerk checked a drawer labeled RECENTLY MISLAID. Inside were two birthdays, a very long lunch break, and the last ten minutes of a detective film.

“Here it is.” She placed three blue hours on the desk. “You'll need to use them before Friday.”

“Can I go back and give my presentation?”

“Oh, no. Past use is strictly prohibited. But you may insert them anywhere ahead.”

Priya considered her calendar. Then she tucked the hours into Thursday evening, between washing the dishes and going to bed.

She spent one reading, one baking something badly, and one doing nothing at all.

On Friday morning, she returned to the department and asked whether they had any more.
