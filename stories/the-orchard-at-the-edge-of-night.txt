---
title: The Orchard at the Edge of Night
tags: fantasy, folklore
date: 2026-01-20
excerpt: The trees only bear fruit after sunset, and each apple contains a dream someone has forgotten.
---
Everyone in the village knew not to enter the orchard after dark.

That was when its apples ripened—silver beneath the moon, humming softly on their branches. If you held one to your ear, you could hear a dream abandoned by someone in the waking world.

Anya went looking for her brother's dream on the longest night of the year.

She carried no lantern. The trees made their own pale light, and the fallen leaves whispered directions beneath her boots. Near the orchard's center, she found an apple that laughed in Tomas's voice.

Inside it, he was running again. He raced over summer grass on two strong legs, shouting for her to catch him.

Anya plucked the apple. At once, every branch in the orchard turned toward her.

“A dream taken,” said a voice among the roots, “must be replaced.”

So Anya closed her eyes and offered the orchard her oldest dream: a little house beyond the hills, with a room that belonged only to her.

In the morning, Tomas woke and stood beside his bed.

Years later, when Anya passed the orchard, she sometimes glimpsed a warm window shining between the trees. She never regretted the trade, but she always waved.
