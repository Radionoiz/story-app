---
title: The Recipe in the Wall
tags: family, contemporary
date: 2025-04-02
excerpt: While renovating her grandmother's kitchen, June discovers a recipe written directly onto the plaster.
---
The recipe appeared when June pulled the old cabinet from the kitchen wall.

It was written in her grandmother's slanted hand, directly on the plaster: two cups flour, one spoon sugar, enough warm water. Beneath that came a list of ingredients no shop carried.

A story you have never told.

The name of someone you miss.

One small forgiveness.

June sat on the dusty floor and read it twice.

She made the bread that afternoon. Into the dough she whispered the story of the summer she had planned to run away. She said her grandmother's name. For the forgiveness, she chose herself.

The loaf rose crooked and golden. Its crust cracked loudly as it cooled.

When June tasted it, she was eight years old again, standing on a chair while her grandmother guided her flour-covered hands. She could hear the radio by the window and rain ticking against the glass.

The memory lasted only a mouthful.

Before the contractors returned, June copied the recipe onto the newly exposed wall in her own handwriting. Then she placed the cabinet back over it for whoever might need it next.
