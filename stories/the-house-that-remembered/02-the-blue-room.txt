---
title: The Blue Room
order: 2
---
The door at the top of the stairs was painted the same faded blue as the house.

Lena opened it onto a summer afternoon.

Her grandmother sat beside the window, younger than Lena had ever known her, sewing a silver button onto a child's coat. Rain shone on the garden even though sunlight filled the room.

“You took your time,” her grandmother said.

Lena forgot how to breathe. “Can you see me?”

“The house can.” Her grandmother bit the thread and held out the coat. One button was still missing. “Memories aren't recordings, darling. They notice who is watching.”

Footsteps sounded in the hall behind Lena.

Her grandmother's expression changed. “Whatever calls your name,” she whispered, “do not open the attic.”
