---
title: The Attic Door
order: 3
---
The voice in the hallway belonged to Lena's mother.

It called once, softly, from behind the attic door.

Lena climbed the final stairs. The silver button from the blue room lay in her palm, warm as a living thing. She understood now why her mother had abandoned the house and why she had left Lena its key.

Some memories asked to be kept. Others asked to be released.

Lena pressed the button into the empty hollow beneath the doorknob. The house exhaled.

Every door opened at once. Laughter, arguments, music, and rain rushed through the halls, then lifted like birds through the roof. The attic fell silent.

By morning, all the clocks agreed.

Lena painted the front door yellow. She kept the key, but from that day forward, it opened only the house.
