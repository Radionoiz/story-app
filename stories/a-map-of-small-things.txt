---
title: A Map of Small Things
tags: literary, memory
date: 2026-05-04
excerpt: An old cartographer begins one final map—not of roads or borders, but of everything he is afraid to forget.
---
When Elias could no longer remember the names of distant capitals, he began mapping what remained.

He drew the blue cup with the chip along its rim. He marked the exact place where the afternoon sun warmed the kitchen floor. In careful ink, he charted the three-note whistle his wife made whenever she came in from the garden.

The map grew across the dining table and down onto the floor. There were no scales, no compass roses, no borders. Only small countries of attention.

His granddaughter found him there one Sunday, asleep among the pages. She did not wake him. Instead, she took up a pencil and added the sound of rain in the gutters, the smell of old books, and the way his hand always found hers when they crossed the street.

Years later, she would unfold the map whenever she needed directions home.
