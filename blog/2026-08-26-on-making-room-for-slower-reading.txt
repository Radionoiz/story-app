---
title: On Making Room for Slower Reading
tags: reading, reflections
date: 2026-08-26
excerpt: Why I wanted this collection to feel less like a feed and more like a shelf you can return to.
---
I wanted this site to have the feeling of finding a book someone left open on a windowsill.

The internet is very good at asking us to move quickly: another tab, another notification, another thing to decide. Stories ask for a different kind of attention. They need a little quiet around them.

That is why the collection is simple. There is no endless scroll, no progress bar insisting on a finish line, and no recommendation engine trying to predict the next mood. There are just stories, arranged like books on a shelf, ready when you are.

The blog will be a place for the things that gather around the stories: process notes, reading lists, stray observations, and perhaps the occasional glimpse of what is coming next.

Thank you for taking the time to be here. I hope you find something worth lingering over.
