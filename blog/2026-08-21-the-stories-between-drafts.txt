---
title: The Stories Between Drafts
tags: writing, behind the scenes
date: 2026-08-21
excerpt: A note about the small, unfinished ideas that quietly shape a longer piece of fiction.
---
Not every story begins with a first sentence.

Sometimes it begins with a color I want to keep looking at, or a room with a window that faces the wrong way. Sometimes it is only a name in the margin of a notebook, waiting patiently for the rest of itself to arrive.

Those fragments are not failures to finish. They are a kind of weather system around the work: little pressures, flashes, and changing temperatures that eventually tell me where to go next.

Lately, I have been keeping them all in one place. A sentence about a woman who repairs umbrellas. A map with no north. A question about what a house might remember after everyone has gone.

Some will become stories. Others will remain beautiful debris. I am learning to be grateful for both.
